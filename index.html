About Me & My Journey

From Classical Grace to High-Fashion Couture

Rooted in Chennai and commanding audiences across South Asia, I bring an arresting synergy of heritage elegance, high-fashion poise, and sharp contemporary editorial aesthetic.

With an intuitive grasp of proportion, silhouette, and camera kinetics, I collaborate with creative directors to transform commercial concepts into memorable visual stories. From leading national campaigns for heritage jewellery houses like Malabar Gold & Diamonds to walking designer runways and crafting avant-garde editorials, my craft is defined by effortless authenticity.

Versatility of Canvas
