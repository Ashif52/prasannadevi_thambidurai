01 About

Grace in every frame

Prasanna Devi is a versatile fashion model based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with a natural ability to transform before the camera. From high-fashion editorial shoots to traditional jewellery campaigns, she brings an effortless elegance and commanding presence to every project.

With experience spanning fashion walks, commercial advertisements, concept photoshoots, and serving as a muse for professional makeup artists, Prasanna embodies the intersection of contemporary style and timeless Indian beauty. Her portfolio reflects a dedication to the craft and an innate understanding of light, movement, and emotion.
